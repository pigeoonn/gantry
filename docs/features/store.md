# The mod store

*Find mods → Store* shows mods of the selected game from **Nexus Mods** and **Thunderstore** as cards, newest data first.

- **Sort chips:** most downloaded (the default), popular lately (most downloaded among mods changed in the last six
  months), most liked, new, recently updated.
- **Source chips:** one site or both. The category box narrows the list; *Hide mods I have* removes what is already in
  your library. The list loads more as you scroll.
- **A card** shows the picture, downloads, likes, size, and how old the latest file is. Click it to read the mod's page
  (description, gallery, what changed, what it needs) before anything is installed.
- **Get it:** Thunderstore mods install by themselves (with the mods they need). With a free Nexus account the mod's page
  opens and Gantry takes the file when you press *Slow download*; Premium accounts download directly.
- **Starter pack:** a short, hand-made list for the games people play most, with a line on why each mod is there.
  *Get all* fetches them one after another.
- **Adult mods** are hidden unless *Settings → Show adult mods in the store* is on.
- Games with anti-cheat show a note instead of mods: Gantry will not put mods there.

Nexus needs your API key (the Nexus window explains where to get it); it stays on your computer.
