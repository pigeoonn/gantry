# Look, layout and languages

*Settings* has:
- **Theme gallery:** dark (Gantry, Midnight, Ember, Forest, Ocean), light (Light, Paper, Mist, Sand), *Follow the system*, or
  *Custom* made from one colour, a roundness and light or dark.
- **Share a theme:** in the custom editor, *Copy as a code* gives a short text (`gantry-theme:…`); a friend pastes it with *Use a code from a friend*.
- **Accent colour**, or *Accent follows the game* (taken from the cover art).
- **Motion:** full, calm, or off. **Compact lists** for more on screen. **Interface size** from 100 % to 150 %.
- **Game page:** choose the top of the page (large with the game's art, compact, a bar), what shows (numbers, the health ring,
  labels), and which tab to start on.
- **Library as covers or rows** with the button next to *Overview*.
- **Language:** English, Romanian, Italian, French, Spanish, Russian, German, Portuguese, Polish, Turkish (top bar).

Text colours are fitted to each theme so they stay readable; see [accessibility](accessibility.md).

**Sounds and rumble** (off by default, *Settings*): *Soft sounds* make a quiet tick on buttons and a tone when something works or fails (made on the spot, no files); *Controller rumble* buzzes a gamepad briefly.
